Proposed Property Management System (PMS)
For Daya Properties Ltd
Objective
To replace manual, fragmented processes with a centralized Property Management System
that streamlines rent collection, tenant communication, landlord reporting, and operational
scaling—while significantly reducing costs and staff workload.
1. Centralized Rent Collection & Billing
Key Features
•
Monthly rent and utility billing
•
Configurable payment deadlines
•
Late fee automation
•
Tenant payment portal
•
Support for Paybill and bank transfers
•
Automatic receipt generation
Pain Points Addressed
Current issues:
•
10–15% late payments
•
Heavy reliance on manual follow-ups
•
Physical visits for enforcement
How PMS fixes this:
•
Tenants receive structured, scheduled reminders
•
Payments and penalties are applied automatically
•
Less dependence on staff intervention
Business Impact:
•
Improved on-time payments
•
Reduced enforcement workload
•
Lower operational costs
